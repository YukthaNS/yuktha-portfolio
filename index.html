Featured Project

Academic project

AI-DRIVEN Insect Recognition for Field Crop Management

Project focus: Recognition of rice crop pests using image processing and deep learning.

Problem: Manual pest inspection can be time-consuming and may lead to identification errors. The project explored automated recognition to support crop monitoring.

Approach: Image preprocessing and feature extraction with a CNN-based approach using CaffeNet architecture, alongside a GUI for uploading or capturing pest images and processing them.

Pests covered: Brown Planthopper (BPH), Rice Ear Bug, and Rice Stem Borer.

Reported result: Project materials reported accuracy of up to 97.9%; results depend on the dataset and evaluation setup.

Future scope: Expand the dataset and pest categories; explore lightweight models such as MobileNet or EfficientNet for mobile/IoT use; add GPS-based pest mapping and cloud alerts.
